7. Legal tint limits in the UK

UK law sets minimum visible light transmission (VLT) levels for the front windscreen and front side windows. We will always advise you on which shades are road-legal:

  • Front windscreen: must allow at least 75% of light through
  • Front side windows: must allow at least 70% of light through
  • Rear side windows and rear windscreen: no legal limit

If you choose a shade for the front of the vehicle that doesn't meet these limits (for example, for show or off-road use), we will fit it only if you understand and accept that the vehicle may not be road-legal. We'll ask you to confirm this in writing before we proceed. Responsibility for road-legality after that point rests with you as the driver.

9. Aftercare

Window film needs about 7–14 days to fully cure. During this period:

  • Don't roll the windows down
  • Don't clean the inside of the windows
  • You may notice a slight haze or small water pockets — this is normal and clears as the film dries

After curing, clean the inside of the glass only with a soft cloth and ammonia-free cleaner. We'll give you full written aftercare instructions on the day.
