How long does a full tint take? +
Most cars are 2–4 hours depending on shape and how many windows. We'll give you a precise time when you book, and you're welcome to wait or drop the car off.
What's the difference between standard and ceramic? +
Standard film is dyed and gives you the look, privacy and 99% UV protection. Ceramic uses nano-particles to also reject heat and infrared — meaning a noticeably cooler cabin, no AC strain, and zero signal interference. If you live in the car, ceramic earns its keep.
Is window tinting legal in the UK? +
Yes — with limits. Front windscreen must let through at least 75% of light, and front side windows at least 70%. Rear windows have no legal limit. We'll always advise you on what's road-legal vs show-only.
Do you offer a warranty? +
Every install comes with a written lifetime warranty against bubbling, peeling, cracking and discolouration. Registered to you, transferable on resale.
How soon can you book me in? +
Often the same week — sometimes the same day if there's a cancellation. Drop us your reg and preferred date and we'll come straight back with options.
Will the tint affect my heated rear window? +
No. Our films are safe over heated elements, antennae and parking sensors. Ceramic films are also non-metallic, so no impact on radio, GPS or keyless entry.
